# ZMBA Website — Product Brief

## Product definition

This is the active official website of the ZhongMei Basketball Association (ZMBA), a student-led basketball association serving the Jinling High School ZhongMei community. ZMBA was founded in 2023 and remains active, generally organizing one to two major basketball events each year.

The product is not archive-first. Historical games, documents, photographs, and leadership records belong here because they support a living organization: its current activity, continuity, and future events.

## Verified organization facts

- Full name: ZhongMei Basketball Association
- Abbreviation: ZMBA
- Tagline: Not just a game. It's a legacy.
- Founded: 2023
- Community: Jinling High School ZhongMei
- Official domain: https://zmbasketball.org
- Founder and 1st Commissioner: Henry Wu / 吴昱贤
- Incumbent Commissioner and 2nd Commissioner: Lucian / 鲁东言
- Confirmed public social platforms: Instagram, Xiaohongshu, Douyin, and Bilibili. Account URLs have not been supplied and must remain unset.

## Product principles

- Lead with current and upcoming ZMBA activity; use history as supporting context.
- Publish only facts supported by a source or explicitly confirmed by ZMBA.
- Model incomplete records honestly. Unknown results, participants, locations, and honors stay unknown until confirmed.
- Do not imitate professional-league features unsupported by real ZMBA data: no fabricated standings, statistics, team systems, schedules, awards, or recaps.
- Protect student privacy. Internal documents, credentials, birthdays, and personal profiles require review before public use.
- Future visual work must derive from actual ZMBA branding, the ZMBA logo, real posters, and real event photography—not a generic blue/gold corporate style.

## First public release

The first real public release implements Home, About, Games, People, Rules, and Media. It presents the current organization before its history, gives Lucian priority as incumbent Commissioner, and uses Henry's founding role as continuity rather than nostalgia.

The release uses a restrained court-side editorial system based on the real ZMBA mark, posters, outdoor-court colors, and event photography. Unknown event details are omitted instead of padded with archive copy or league-style statistics. Rules are classified on the site but are not downloadable until publication approval is confirmed. Social platform names may appear, but their links remain unset.
