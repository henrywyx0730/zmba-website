# ZMBA Website — Product Brief

## Organization

ZMBA stands for ZhongMei Basketball Association.

Founded in May 2023, ZMBA was created as a student-led basketball association with its own games, rules, leadership, media, records, and traditions.

## Website Mission

The website should preserve ZMBA history while presenting the organization as a real, structured basketball association.

It should not feel like a generic school club website.

It should combine:

- an official league website
- a historical archive
- a media gallery
- a record of leadership and institutional continuity

## Core Themes

- Community
- Fairness
- Respect
- Inclusion
- Competition
