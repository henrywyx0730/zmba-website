# ZMBA Design Direction

## CTC-level restraint, ZMBA-specific character

The homepage uses the same design discipline as a mature institutional site while remaining unmistakably ZMBA. Its authority comes from clear information, real basketball records, and the association itself rather than sports-template styling.

- **System:** light-first, with a soft paper-white surface, near-black text, pale neutral rules, and restrained ZMBA blue.
- **Typography:** one clean contemporary sans-serif relationship. Scale, weight, spacing, and alignment create hierarchy without condensed sports display type.
- **Layout:** one disciplined centered frame, cardless by default, with thin rules and generous but practical spacing.
- **Images:** real ZMBA photography provides the basketball identity. The actual shield is the brand anchor, and photographs remain clean, rectangular, and factual.
- **Motion:** existing hover, focus, and mobile-menu feedback only. No decorative motion.

## Explicitly rejected

This direction intentionally rejects both corporate or luxury prestige aesthetics and black, condensed sports-editorial templates. It also excludes gradients, glass, shadows, rounded-card grids, stock basketball imagery, and generic sports-site vocabulary.
