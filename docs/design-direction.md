# ZMBA Design Direction

## Court record, still in play

The site should feel like an active student-run basketball association documenting itself from courtside. The visual system comes from the real ZMBA shield, the compressed game-poster headlines, outdoor court geometry, school uniforms and jerseys, direct group photographs, and the existing ZMBA photo watermark.

- **Tone:** basketball-first, youthful, editorial, direct, and current.
- **Layout:** asymmetric image-led compositions with sharp edges, date-led chronology, and varied portrait/landscape ratios. The rhythm should resemble a well-made game program rather than a component library.
- **Typography:** a compact, heavy sans-serif display stack inspired by the All-Star poster, paired with a plain readable sans-serif for body copy. No prestige serif.
- **Color:** off-white and charcoal provide the page surface. ZMBA blue is the single interface accent. Gold remains primarily inside the real shield; court red and green remain in photography rather than becoming decorative UI colors.
- **Images:** use selected real ZMBA photography at meaningful scale. Keep crops purposeful, preserve varied aspect ratios, and use neutral captions when context is incomplete.
- **Motion:** restrained feedback and state transitions only. Navigation, buttons, and the mobile-menu state may move; the photographs and page itself remain calm.
- **Signature:** court-line framing and strong date typography create continuity between event records without pretending the records are complete.

## Explicitly rejected

No dark corporate gradient, luxury-gold treatment, glassmorphism, rounded-card grids, fake league statistics, stock basketball imagery, NBA imitation, or generic motivational copy.
